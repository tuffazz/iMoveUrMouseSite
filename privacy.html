Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

iMoveUrMouse ("the App") is a macOS menu bar utility developed and published on the Mac App Store. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your rights in relation to that information.

Information We Collect

We collect no personal information. The App does not collect, transmit, or share any data about you or your device. The only data stored by the App is a single on/off preference (isEnabled) saved locally to your Mac using the macOS standard UserDefaults system. This value:

  • Never leaves your device.
  • Is not synced to iCloud or any remote service.
  • Contains no personal or identifying information.

Network Activity

The App makes no network requests of any kind. It has no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no advertising framework, and no third-party libraries. You can verify this: iMoveUrMouse operates entirely offline.

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or transfer any data — because no data is collected in the first place. There are no third-party services, advertising networks, or data brokers involved.

System Permissions

The App does not request any system permissions, including Accessibility access, Location, Contacts, Camera, Microphone, or any other entitlement that would grant access to personal data. The only system capability the App uses is an IOPMAssertion — a standard macOS API for preventing display sleep — which requires no user permission prompt and accesses no personal data.

Children's Privacy

Because the App collects no data from any user, there is no distinction based on age. iMoveUrMouse does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated, the new version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Because no data is collected, changes are expected to be minimal or non-existent.
