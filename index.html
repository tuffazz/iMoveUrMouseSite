How it works

Simple, deep, and invisible.

When you enable iMoveUrMouse, it quietly tells macOS to hold the system idle timer at zero — the same low-level signal that prevents your display from dimming during a movie or a video call.

Microsoft Teams, Slack, and Zoom check this system timer — not your keyboard or mouse — to decide whether you're active. By holding the timer, iMoveUrMouse keeps your status green without moving the cursor, posting fake input events, or asking for any special access.

Flip the toggle in the menu bar to enable. Flip it again to stop. That's it.
